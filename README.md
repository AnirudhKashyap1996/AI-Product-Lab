# AI Product Lab

A hands-on learning and portfolio repository documenting my journey into
AI Product Management.

This repository combines AI fundamentals, product management thinking,
hands-on experimentation, and AI-powered prototypes focused initially on
enterprise operations and intelligent systems.

## 🎯 Purpose

My goal is to build practical AI Product Management skills by:

- Understanding AI and ML concepts from first principles
- Learning how AI systems are designed and evaluated
- Translating customer problems into AI product opportunities
- Understanding AI product architecture and technical trade-offs
- Building small AI prototypes
- Practicing product discovery, prioritization, roadmapping, and metrics
- Documenting learnings and product decisions

## 🧠 Learning Areas

### AI Product Management

- Problem definition
